GTCO plans rights issue to meet new capital requirements

Guaranty Trust Holding Company (GTCO) said it will raise fresh capital through a rights issue to meet the revised minimum capital requirements set by the Central Bank of Nigeria for commercial banks.

The group said the capital raise would be executed in phases and is subject to approval by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and existing shareholders.

Market watchers expect several tier-one banks to follow with similar capital-raising plans, which could weigh on near-term share prices on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) even as the sector's long-term capital buffers improve.
