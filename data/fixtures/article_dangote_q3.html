Dangote Cement reports strong Q3 earnings on higher volumes

Dangote Cement Plc (DANGCEM) reported higher revenue and profit for the third quarter, helped by stronger volumes and resilient pricing in Nigeria and across its pan-African operations.

The company said earnings benefited from improved energy supply and cost control, even as the naira's movement against the dollar added pressure to input costs.

The board is expected to consider an interim dividend, which would be the second payout this year, subject to shareholder approval at the next meeting.
