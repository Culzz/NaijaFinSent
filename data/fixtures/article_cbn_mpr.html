CBN holds MPR at 27.5% as inflation eases in September

By Fixture Reporter

The Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Monetary Policy Committee voted to hold the Monetary Policy Rate (MPR) at 27.5 percent, citing a slower rise in headline inflation and relative stability in the foreign-exchange market.

The committee said the naira had firmed at the official window, trading near 1,540 to the dollar, while the NGX All-Share Index advanced on improved sentiment among banking stocks such as GTCO and Zenith Bank.

Analysts noted that the decision gives the CBN room to monitor the impact of earlier tightening on credit to the real sector, including cement and telecoms firms like Dangote Cement and MTN Nigeria.
